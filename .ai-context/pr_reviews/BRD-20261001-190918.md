## Conditions of Approval (BRD-001.D71)
The BRD is approved as functionally development-ready. Development proceeds subject to each of the following Technical decisions being defined in the feature `plan.md` and approved with TL (Subhajit Mukherjee) concurrence at Gate 1. Development of an affected area must not start before its item is approved:

1. Database mechanism for the single-active-request rule (D07). A plain unique constraint on employee and status is not acceptable.
2. Adapter contract (D69) with the idempotency model (D53), cancel and reverse handling (D54, D55) and callback handling (D56).
3. Callback authentication scheme (D57).
4. Audit hash chain and verification (D64), and the database permission model for the audit table.
5. Scheduler: effective-date run, recovery check, SLA jobs and their serialisation with completion processing (D60, D62, D43).
6. Timezone handling: UTC storage, Asia/Kolkata display and calculation (D31).
7. Server-side field filtering by role (D65).

These are Gate 1 / plan conditions, not BRD blockers.
